PHASE 4: The Hands-On Trades Verification

The NCCER Core Curriculum is an industry-standard credential issued by the National Center for Construction Education & Research.

Under national rules, an official OSHA 10 card automatically replaces the first 10 hours of the NCCER CORE textbook curriculum and exempts you from having to take the written NCCER Basic Safety test. Single-day testing format is not a public class and is instead handled as an Employer Testing Day.

This single day includes Basic Safety Check, Hand & Power Tools Assessment, Basic Rigging & Signaling Assessment, and Construction Math & Material Handling.

I understand that this NCCER CORE curriculum will be combined into a 4-6 hour single day private employee testing day requested by my employer.
